# Emergency Food Network Demand Analysis & Forecasting

This project analyzes multi-year service and food distribution data from Emergency Food Network (EFN) to better understand client demand, service utilization, and operational patterns across service units.

## Project Overview

The analysis combines data validation, exploratory data analysis, and a limited time series forecasting extension. The goal is to present a clear, professional, and decision-relevant view of how demand and distribution patterns evolved over time, while also showing what can and cannot be inferred from the available data.

## Objectives

- Validate the structure and internal consistency of the dataset
- Analyze service reach, repeat utilization, and food distribution trends
- Examine variation across service units and demographic patterns
- Explore forecasting methods to assess future demand under real-world data limitations
- Communicate findings clearly for both technical and non-technical audiences

## Methods

- Data validation and consistency checks
- Exploratory data analysis
- Descriptive statistical interpretation
- Time series analysis
- Forecasting and diagnostic review
